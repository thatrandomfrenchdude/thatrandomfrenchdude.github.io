I've recently begun teaching a regular, all-levels yoga class on Friday at 6pm, and I thought it would be fun to share the flows I create for my students. Hope you find this fun, challenging, and relaxing!<br><br>

### Warm-Up
**Tadasana (Mountain Pose)** - close your eyes, take a few deep breaths, and set an intention for your practice
<br>**Ardha Surya Namaskar (Half Sun Salutation)** - 4 rounds
<br>**Utkatasana (Chair Pose)** - 2 rounds. First pass is normal: lift arms overhead, bend knees, sit back as if sitting in a chair. On the second pass, lift your heels off the ground to engage calves and core, and lower down as much as comfortable
<br><br>

### Opening Flow
**Surya Namaskar A (Sun Salutation A)** - 3 rounds
<br>**Surya Namaskar B (Sun Salutation B)** - 2 rounds
<br>**Malasana (Squat)** - move into crow pose if comfortable, and flow through an optional vinyasa back to standing
<br>**Padangusthasana (Hand to Big Toe Pose)** - from standing, fold forward to grab your big toe with your index and middle finger and hold
<br>**Padahastasana (Hand to Foot Pose)** - from standing, fold forward and place your hands under your feet, gently stretch the hamstrings
<br>**Tadasana (Mountain Pose)** - return to standing, take a few deep breaths<br><br>

### Standing Sequence 1
**Warrior I (Virabhadrasana I)** - from standing, step one foot back and bend the front knee to 90 degrees, square hips to the front
<br>**Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II)** - open hips and arms to the side, gaze over the front hand
<br>**Reverse Warrior (Viparita Virabhadrasana)** - lean your torso back from Warrior II, reach the front arm overhead and back, sliding the back hand down the back leg
<br>**Extended Side Angle (Utthita Parsvakonasana)** - return from Reverse Warrior, then lean forward and bring the front forearm to the front thigh or hand to the ground inside or outside the front foot, extending the top arm overhead
<br>**Triangle Pose (Trikonasana)** - straighten the front leg from Extended Side Angle, reach forward with the front hand, and lower it to the shin, ankle, or ground while extending the top arm overhead<br><br>

### Standing Rest
<br>**Prasarita Padottanasana C (Wide-Legged Forward Bend)** - clasp hands behind back to open shoulders, fold forward, and let head hang heavy
<br>**Prasarita Padottanasana A (Wide-Legged Forward Bend)** - bend forward and let your hands come to the ground
    - option to walk hands to the right/left sides one at a time and drop into a squat on each side with one leg extended<br><br>

### Standing Sequence 2
<br>**Warrior I (Virabhadrasana I)** - from standing, step one foot back and bend the front knee to 90 degrees, square hips to the front
<br>**Baddha Virabhadrasana (Humble Warrior)** - from Warrior I, interlace fingers behind back, fold forward while keeping hips square
<br>**Anjaneyasana (High Lunge)** - raise back up from humble warrior, pivot the back heel up off the ground, and reach arms overhead
<br>**Prayer Twist** - from high lunge, bring hands to prayer position at heart center, twist toward the bent knee side, and bring the opposite elbow to the outside of the bent knee
<br>**Standing Twist** - from the prayer twist, strengthen the foundation and rise up to standing while keeping the twist
<br>**Open Twist** - from the standing twist, open to the other side for a counter twist
<br>**Anjaneyasana (High Lunge -> Low Lunge)** - arrive back in high lunge, then lower the back knee to the ground for low lunge to stretch the hip flexors
<br>**Hanumanasana (Half Split)** - from low lunge, shift hips back and straighten the front leg to stretch the hamstrings
<br>**Hanumanasana (Full Split)** - if comfortable, slowly slide into full split, using blocks under your hands or hips for support if needed
<br>**Tadasana (Mountain Pose)**
<br>**Vrksasana L/R (Tree Pose)** - balance on one leg, place the sole of the other foot on the inner thigh or calf, avoid the knee<br><br>

### Inversions
Practice one or more of the following inversions, using a wall for support if needed:
<br>**Adho Mukha Vrksasana (Handstand)**
<br>**Pincha Mayurasana (Forearm Stand)**
<br>**Sirsasana (Headstand)**
<br>**Salamba Sarvangasana (Shoulderstand)**
<br>**Halasana (Plow Pose)**
<br>**Legs-Up-The-Wall Pose (Viparita Karani)**<br><br>

### Cool Down
**Dandasana (Staff Pose)** - sit with legs extended, flex feet, and engage the core
<br>**Paschimottanasana (Seated Forward Bend)** - fold forward over extended legs, reach for your feet or shins
<br>**Janu Sirsasana L/R (Head-to-Knee Forward Bend)** - bend one knee, place the sole of the foot against the inner thigh of the extended leg, and fold forward over the extended leg
<br>**Baddha Konasana (Cobbler Pose)** - bring the soles of your feet together, let your knees fall open, and gently press them toward the ground
<br>**Setu Bandhasana (Bridge Pose)** - 2 rounds. lie on your back, bend your knees, place your feet hip-width apart, and lift your hips toward the ceiling
<br>**Dhanyurasana (Bow Pose)** - lie on your back, bend your knees like bridge pose, tuck your hands under your shoulders, and press through your arms and legs to lift your chest and thighs off the ground
<br>**Reclined Pigeon Pose L/R** - bring one knee toward your chest, cross the ankle over the opposite thigh, and pull the uncrossed leg toward your chest to stretch the hip
<br>**Reclined Twist L/R** - lie on your back. bring your knees to your chest, then drop them to one side so your hips are stacked with your shoulders on the ground, extend your arms out to a T, and turn your head to the opposite side
<br>**Happy Baby Pose** - lie on your back, bend your knees, grab the outer edges of your feet with your hands, and gently pull your knees toward the ground
<br>**Savasana (Corpse Pose)** - lie flat on your back, let your feet fall open, arms relaxed at your sides, close your eyes, and focus on your breath for at least 5 minutes
